Vinasétan Ratheil Houndji holds a joint PhD in engineering sciences from UCLouvain and Université d'Abomey-Calavi, awarded in 2017. His research concerns constraint programming, combinatorial optimisation and machine learning.

He applies these methods to problems in health, agriculture, education and logistics. His work includes teaching at IFRI, supervising students and coordinating research projects with partner institutions.

## Responsibilities

- Head of the Software Engineering Department at IFRI
- Coordinator of IFRI's bachelor's and master's programmes
- President of FRIARE
- President of the Benin Workshop on Artificial Intelligence
