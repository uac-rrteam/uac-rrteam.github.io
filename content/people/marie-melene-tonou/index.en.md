Marie Mélène Sèmèvo Tonou is a PhD student and research assistant at LRSIA. She completed her engineering degree at the École Nationale Supérieure de Génie Mathématique et Modélisation, UNSTIM, Benin.

Her work combines health-data analysis and machine learning. She also volunteers at FRIARE and teaches machine learning and data analysis part time.

## Research topic

Her PhD concerns the automatic recognition of epileptic seizure types from electroencephalograms. It includes the construction of a local EEG corpus and the study of methods suited to these data.
