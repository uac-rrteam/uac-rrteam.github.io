Marianne Omonlola Balogoun is a PhD student in information and communication technologies. Supported by a PASET-RSIF scholarship, she studies machine-learning models of electromagnetic wave propagation in wireless communication networks.

Her work covers digital terrestrial television and technologies from 5G to 6G and beyond. She is also interested in the ethics, responsibility and safety of artificial intelligence.
