# Join the Ratheil Research Team

The **Ratheil Research Team** is based within the **Laboratory for Research in Computer Science and Applications (LRSIA)** at the **Institute for Training and Research in Computer Science (IFRI)**, located in the **Polyvalent Building, University of Abomey-Calavi Campus, BP 526 Cotonou, Benin**.

The team supervises undergraduate projects, research internships and doctoral work in artificial intelligence, constraint programming and optimisation. Available topics depend on current projects and supervision capacity.

## Research Interns and Students

Undergraduate and graduate students from the **University of Abomey-Calavi (UAC)** and other institutions may apply for research placements. Current topics include combinatorial optimisation, machine learning, health-data analysis, embedded systems and software tools for research.

Most projects require programming experience, often in Python, and a foundation in mathematics, algorithms or data analysis. Additional requirements are specified for each topic.

## Visiting Researchers

Visiting researchers and self-funded interns may contact the team with a proposed topic, intended dates and expected source of funding. Requests are considered according to their fit with ongoing work and the availability of a supervisor.

## Collaboration

Institutional collaborations are considered when they define a clear research question, the contributions of each partner and, where relevant, the conditions governing data access.

Enquiries should be sent to **Ratheil Houndji** at **[vratheilhoundji@gmail.com](mailto:vratheilhoundji@gmail.com)** with a CV and a brief description of the proposed work.
