The **Ratheil Research Team** brings together faculty, PhD candidates and students at the Laboratory for Research in Computer Science and Applications (**LRSIA**). It is led by **Dr Vinasétan Ratheil Houndji** at the Institute for Training and Research in Computer Science (**IFRI**), University of Abomey-Calavi.

Its research programme, **Artificial Intelligence for Societal Impact**, covers machine learning, constraint programming and combinatorial optimisation. Research questions are drawn from problems encountered in Benin and in settings where data, infrastructure or computing resources are limited.

Current applications include health, agriculture, education and logistics. Depending on the project, the team studies data quality, model interpretability, fairness in algorithmic decisions or the solution of planning problems.

## Training and collaboration

The team supervises dissertations, research internships and doctoral work. Projects generally require a background in computer science or mathematics and some programming experience; specific prerequisites vary by topic.

Collaborations involve university groups, hospital departments, public bodies and industry partners when their data or expertise is relevant to the research.
