## Our areas

### Learning from data

The team studies prediction, classification and the analysis of signals or images. Its work covers data preparation as well as model evaluation and interpretation.

### Constraint programming

Constraint programming is used to model problems in which many rules must hold simultaneously. Research covers constraint propagation, search strategies and model design.

### Combinatorial optimisation

Problems include vehicle routing, resource allocation, timetabling and scheduling. They are addressed through mathematical programming, exact methods and metaheuristics.

### Responsible artificial intelligence

Interpretability, fairness and data quality are examined whenever models contribute to a decision. Particular attention is paid to the limitations of locally available datasets.
