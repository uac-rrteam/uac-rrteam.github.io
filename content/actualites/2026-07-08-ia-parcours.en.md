Workshop delivered during the launch of the University of Abomey-Calavi's artificial intelligence strategy. Designed for students, it covers responsible academic use of generative AI, prompt design, bias, hallucinations, confidentiality, disclosure of AI use, and the rights and obligations set by UAC's ethical framework.
