titre: Responsible governance of artificial intelligence in African higher education
date: 2026-07-20
periode: July 2026

Talk delivered at the “AI, Education and Ethics” symposium of the Permanent Commission on Education and Ethics of Benin's National Academy of Sciences, Arts and Letters. It proposes governance principles, institutional mechanisms and an operational cycle covering risk levels, data sovereignty, academic integrity, auditing and avenues for appeal.
