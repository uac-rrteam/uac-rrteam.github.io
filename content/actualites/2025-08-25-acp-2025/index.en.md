---
titre: 20th ACP Summer School, first African edition
date: 2025-08-25
periode: August 2025
---

From 25 to 29 August 2025, IFRI hosted the twentieth Association for Constraint Programming Summer School, organised by Ratheil Houndji and John Aoga. Fifty people attended, 86% of them from African institutions. Fourteen full scholarships and thirty partial scholarships were awarded.
