From 25 to 29 August 2025, IFRI hosted the twentieth Association for Constraint Programming Summer School. Its theme was **Constraint Programming for Sustainable Development**.

Lectures and practical sessions covered modelling, methods for solving combinatorial problems and applications to sustainable development.

The school was chaired by Ratheil Houndji and John Aoga.
