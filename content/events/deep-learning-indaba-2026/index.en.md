Poster GP-141, presented at Deep Learning Indaba 2026, concerns multi-product vehicle routing with changeover costs.

It describes an optimisation formulation for logistics operations in which vehicles carry several products and each product change incurs a preparation cost.

The formulation, test instances and experimental platform are described on the [MPVRP-CC page](/en/research/projects/mpvrp-cc).
