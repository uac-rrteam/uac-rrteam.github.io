The sixth **Benin Workshop on Artificial Intelligence** is scheduled to take place at IFRI from 23 to 27 November 2026. BWAI is an annual meeting devoted to artificial-intelligence work conducted in Benin and the wider region.

The programme includes research communications, posters, demonstrations, workshops and tutorials. It is open to researchers, students and organisations presenting a project or a case study.

Several members of the Ratheil Research Team serve on the scientific, programme and logistics committees. Times and registration details are published on the event website.
