OpenCS4Dev is a cooperation project between UCLouvain and Université d'Abomey-Calavi. It concerns the production and sharing of open teaching material for computer science courses.

Ratheil Houndji coordinates the southern partnership. Work includes assessing needs, adapting material to the programmes concerned and arranging for its maintenance by the partner institutions.
