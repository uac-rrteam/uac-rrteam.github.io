ForCES concerns continuing professional development in digital pedagogy for higher-education teachers. Its first phase documents current policies and practices in Benin and Senegal; the findings inform the design of the training scheme.

The consortium includes Université d'Abomey-Calavi, UCLouvain and Université Cheikh Anta Diop. Ratheil Houndji coordinates the activities conducted in Benin.
