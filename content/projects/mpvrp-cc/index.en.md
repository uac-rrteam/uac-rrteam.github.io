The multi-product vehicle routing problem with changeover costs arises in petroleum, chemical and food distribution. A fleet must deliver several products from several depots, while every product change between trips requires cleaning and preparation.

The team proposes a mixed-integer linear programming formulation that jointly handles multiple depots, heterogeneous fleets, split deliveries, stock constraints, multiple trips and changeover costs.

The model is evaluated with Gurobi on synthetic instances. An open platform supports instance generation, visualisation, feasibility checking and comparison with future exact or metaheuristic methods.
