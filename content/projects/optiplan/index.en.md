Opti'Plan addresses the scheduling of dissertation defences in large university programmes. A timetable must simultaneously satisfy the availability of examiners, candidates, rooms and time slots.

The project studied how to model these rules and solve the resulting problem with optimisation methods. It ran from 2024 to 2025 as part of an ARES-funded partnership.
