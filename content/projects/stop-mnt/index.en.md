STOP-MNT is a project on the prevention of non-communicable diseases in Benin, funded by the Université d'Abomey-Calavi Competitive Research Fund.

The Ratheil Research Team leads the second work package, which covers artificial intelligence and connected devices. It examines how data and connected devices can contribute to the prevention activities planned by the project.
