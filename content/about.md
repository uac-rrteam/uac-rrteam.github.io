The **Ratheil Research Team** brings together faculty, PhD candidates and students at the **Laboratory for Research in Computer Science and Applications (LRSIA)**. It is led by **Dr Vinasétan Ratheil Houndji** at the **Institute for Training and Research in Computer Science (IFRI)**, **University of Abomey-Calavi (UAC)**, Benin.

The team's programme, **Artificial Intelligence for Societal Impact**, covers machine learning, constraint programming and combinatorial optimisation. Current applications include crop modelling and plant-disease detection; cardiovascular and kidney disease; EEG analysis; learning analytics; vehicle routing; and university timetabling.

Projects are conducted with academic groups, hospital departments, public institutions and industry partners when their data or expertise is relevant. The team also supervises undergraduate projects, research internships and doctoral work.
