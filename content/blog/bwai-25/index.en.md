The **Benin Workshop on Artificial Intelligence** (BWAI) is an annual national
workshop that promotes intelligent systems made in Africa, research and
practice in artificial intelligence, and scientific exchange among
researchers, practitioners and engineers.

Dr Ratheil Houndji serves as General Co-Chair alongside Professor Eugène C.
Ezin, President.

## Programme

The programme combines technical talks, practical demonstrations, posters,
student abstracts and thematic workshops.

## Previous editions

- BWAI-21, 20–24 December 2021, UAC.
- BWAI-22, third edition, 19–23 December 2022, UAC.
- BWAI-24, fourth edition, 21–25 May 2024, UAC.
- BWAI-25, fifth edition, 17–21 November 2025, UAC.
